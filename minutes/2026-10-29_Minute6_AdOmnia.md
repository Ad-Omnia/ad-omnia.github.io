## Discussion and Decisions

### Pending Work

The team reviewed the status of the outstanding tasks. All work has been assigned and is on track.

**Decision:** The pending deliverables will be completed within the next few days.

### SIRESP Desafio Inovação 2026 Report

Following the decision to participate in the SIRESP Desafio Inovação 2026, the team discussed the report required for the submission, which has a maximum length of 20 pages.

**Decision:** The report will be split evenly among the team members, with each member writing a maximum of 4 pages.

### User Reports Feature

The team discussed a Waze-style feature that would allow citizens to report occurrences through the public application, weighing its value against the current scope and the remaining development time.

**Decision:** The feature will be set aside for now. It may be implemented later as a plugin.

### Partnership with Origami Hub

The team established a new partnership with the company Origami Hub.

**Decision:** Origami Hub is now a partner of the project.

### Public/Private Documentation

The team discussed the possibility of hiding the documentation of the private application, given that it is intended for internal use in the control room. The matter was brought to the supervisor.

**Decision:** After consulting the supervisor, all project documentation, including that of the private application, will remain public.

### Repository Organization

With development about to intensify, the team discussed how to structure the codebase across repositories, taking into account that the project has two separate client applications.

**Decision:** The frontend and backend will be split into separate repositories, with two frontend repositories: one for the public application and another for the private application.
