Student numbers are not published. People outside the team who take part in a meeting are referred to by their role, unless they agree to be named.

## Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub receives your IP address when you open a page and may keep it in its logs for security purposes, under the [GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement){ target="_blank" rel="noopener" }. GitHub is a US company; transfers to the US rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework ([Decision (EU) 2023/1795](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dec_impl/2023/1795/oj){ target="_blank" rel="noopener" }). The team does not see or keep these logs. The repository link at the top of every page also asks GitHub's API (`api.github.com`) for the repository's public star and fork counts, so GitHub receives your IP address from that request too.

Fonts and the PDF viewer used for the slides and the brand book are served from this site, so opening a page does not contact any other provider.

## What is stored on your device

The site keeps your light or dark theme choice and whether the side panels are collapsed in your browser's local storage. This stays on your device, is never sent to anyone and is strictly necessary for a feature you chose, so no consent is needed ([Art. 5(3) ePrivacy Directive](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2002/58/oj){ target="_blank" rel="noopener" }, [Art. 5 Lei n.º 41/2004](https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/detalhe/lei/41-2004-480710){ target="_blank" rel="noopener" }). Clearing your browser data removes it.

## Videos

Brand videos are embedded from `youtube-nocookie.com` and load only when you press play. Until then nothing is sent to YouTube. Once you play a video, YouTube receives your IP address under its own privacy policy.

## How long the data is kept

Team, supervisor and meeting data stays on the site for the duration of the project and as part of its public record afterwards, unless the person asks for it to be removed. Removed data may remain in the history of the site's Git repository.

## Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict its use or to object to it, and you can withdraw your consent at any time ([Arts. 15–21 GDPR](https://gdpr-info.eu/art-15-gdpr/){ target="_blank" rel="noopener" }, [Art. 7(3) GDPR](https://gdpr-info.eu/art-7-gdpr/){ target="_blank" rel="noopener" }). Write to <adomnia.inperpetuum@gmail.com>. You can also complain to the Portuguese supervisory authority, the [Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD)](https://www.cnpd.pt/){ target="_blank" rel="noopener" }.
