## Reports and presentations

According to the "Communication Plan" slide, the project's reports/presentations are shared through 4 channels:

1. **Website** — includes an overview of the project, documentation and links to the repositories.
2. **Events** — participation in events: exhibitions and competitions (e.g., Think Tank)
3. **Students@DETI** — presentation with a demonstration of the project.
4. **Other** — meetings with emergency and security agencies.

Internally we communicate via:

- **WhatsApp**: Communication between the Team and the Advisors
- **Discord Server**: Communication between Team members
- **GitHub Projects**: Backlog management
