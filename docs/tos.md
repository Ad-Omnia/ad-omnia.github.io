# **Ad Omnia: Terms and Conditions of Use**

Nov 1, 2026 

## **1\. About Ad Omnia and these Terms**

Ad Omnia is a proof-of-concept civil protection command and response platform, and these Terms govern your use of it.

Ad Omnia brings incident, resource and alert data from several agencies and external sources onto one operational map. It is operated by The Ad Omnia team \- reachable at adomnia.inperpetuum@gmail.com  *\-* as a project of the University of Aveiro and Instituto de Telecomunicações.

There are two versions of the Ad Omnia application, which will be referred to throughout these Terms by the following designations:

* **Public application:** a publicly available map that displays the location of active emergencies and crisis scenarios, as well as any details free from any classification and/or restriction and deemed relevant to the public.  
* **Restricted application:** a private application destined to be used exclusively by authorised civil protection and emergency personnel, both during and outside the occurrence of crisis scenarios, for operational control and data analysis. It or the restricted data that it processes and/or produces is not to be accessed by the general public.

By accessing or using either version of the Ad Omnia application, you accept these Terms. If you do not accept them, do not use any version of Ad Omnia.

## **2\. Not an emergency service**

Ad Omnia does not replace 112 or any official emergency channel. If you or someone else is in danger, call 112 immediately.

The public map is an information aid, not an alert system. An incident missing from the map does not mean an area is safe. Always follow instructions from civil protection authorities, the GNR, firefighters and other official sources over anything shown in Ad Omnia. The public map does not serve as a substitute for direct statements made by emergency authorities.

## **3\. Who may use Ad Omnia**

Anyone may use the public application, while the control room application is restricted to people authorised by their organisation.

* **Public users:** no account is needed.  
* **Authorised users:** control room operators, emergency analysts and other personnel granted access by the Ad Omnia team or by their agency. Access is personal and can be withdrawn at any time, be it by the agency responsible for the individual or directly by the Ad Omnia team in case the individual is found guilty of any sort of breach of these Terms.

Authorised users confirm that they are entitled to handle the data they enter or view, including any classified or restricted information.

## **4\. The public application**

The public map shows information as last received, and it is not guaranteed to be real time.

* An incident appears on the public map only after an authorised operator publishes it. Operators can hide or end it at any time.  
* Data may be delayed, incomplete or corrected later. Where shown, the "last updated" time tells you how current an item is.  
* Published incidents and warnings are created by authorised operators. Ad Omnia does not decide what counts as an emergency.  
* Map markers show the general area of an incident. Do not use them for navigation or to plan an evacuation.

## **5\. The control room application**

Authorised users are responsible for how they use the platform and for the decisions they take with it.

* **Accounts and stations:** access requires authentication of the control room station and the user. Keep your credentials secret, do not share your account, and report suspected misuse promptly.  
* **Authorised use only:** use Ad Omnia only for civil protection and emergency-management work you are entitled to do.  
* **Human decisions:** Ad Omnia supports decisions and does not take them. Operators decide what to create, update, assign, order, publish or close.  
  * **Logging and audit:** incident changes, resource assignments, orders, publications and responses to recommendations are recorded in a decision log and audit trail, with the user and the time. Records are kept for 5 years and may be exported for reporting.  
* **Publishing:** publish incidents and warnings only if you are authorised to, and keep them accurate.  
* **Field responders:** orders registered in Ad Omnia do not reach field teams through the app. Communicate them through your normal channels.

## **6\. Predictions, recommendations and simulations**

Predictions and recommendations are advisory estimates, and people remain responsible for every decision.

* **Predictions:** forecasts of how an incident may progress, or where risk is high, come from statistical and machine-learning models. They can be wrong. Each comes with a confidence indicator, and low confidence calls for extra caution.  
* **Recommendations:** suggestions such as resource allocation or routing can be accepted, adjusted or rejected. Your choice is logged.  
* **Simulations:** scenarios run on historical and synthetic data are planning aids. They are not forecasts of real events and do not affect ongoing operations.  
* **Proof-of-concept status:** models may be trained or tested on synthetic or simulated data and have not been validated for operational use.

## **7\. External data sources and plugins**

Ad Omnia relies on third-party data and cannot guarantee its accuracy or availability.

* **External sources:** data is ingested automatically from sources such as Fogos.pt, by use of their APIs. These sources have their own terms and Ad Omnia does not control them nor does it influence its control in any way. A change or loss of access can interrupt the features that depend on them.  
* **Source status:** Ad Omnia may show when data was last updated and when a source connection is unhealthy.  
* **Restricted data:** some data used in the control room is classified and/or restricted for use and distribution in operational and/or analysis environments. Such data is therefore never made available on the public end of the application.  
* **Plugins:** plugins extend the platform and run in a sandboxed environment, separate from the core system. A plugin may have its own additional terms, in addition to the terms of the Ad Omnia core system.
