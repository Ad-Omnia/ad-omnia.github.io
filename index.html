    Ad Omnia in perpetuum

    To everything, forever.

    Discover
    01

    Converge

    See everything.

    A crisis never arrives through a single channel. Sensors, cameras, weather, social media, reports from the field: each source is an isolated ray. The lens takes them all in and brings them into one beam, a common operational picture.

    02

    Reflect

    Anticipate.

    The beam touches the top of the lens and turns, leaving at the same angle it arrived. This is the turning point: predictive AI reads what is happening as what is about to happen, in time to change the outcome.

    03

    Bend

    Act.

    Inside the lens the ray bends, like the crossbar of the A. Seeing and predicting only matter if they lead to a decision: clear, justified recommendations on where to send resources, aimed at the people on the ground.

    04

    Return

    To everything, forever.

    The beam enters the last lens and opens out again into many rays, each through its own opening, past the edge of the page. One picture, handed back to everyone who needs it, without stopping.

    The project

    A unified operational command and predictive intelligence platform for crisis and emergency management.

    Built for large events, public safety and crisis response. It fuses heterogeneous real-time data into a single Common Operational Picture (COP) and uses predictive AI to anticipate risk and recommend how to allocate resources.

    Current phase: Elaboration (MS2)

    Informatics Engineering Project (43675)
    University of Aveiro, 2026/2027

    Objectives

    What we are building.

    1. 01

      Unified data ecosystem

      Open data and real-time feeds fused into a single knowledge base.

    2. 02

      Predictive AI engine

      Risk anticipation with models trained on synthetic data.

    3. 03

      Low-latency COP interface

      A common operational picture, always current, for the people deciding on the ground.

    4. 04

      Tactical resource recommendation

      Automatic suggestions for allocating resources against the identified risk.

    5. 05

      Modular dual-use architecture

      Independent components, reusable in both civil and defence contexts.
