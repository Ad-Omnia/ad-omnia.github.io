The project

A unified operational command and predictive intelligence platform for crisis and emergency management.

Built for large events, public safety and crisis response. It fuses heterogeneous real-time data into a single Common Operational Picture (COP) and uses predictive AI to anticipate risk and recommend how to allocate resources.

Current phase: Inception (MS1)

Informatics Engineering Project (43675)
University of Aveiro, 2026/2027

Objectives

What we are building.

  1. 01

    Unified data ecosystem

    Open data and real-time feeds fused into a single knowledge base.

  2. 02

    Predictive AI engine

    Risk anticipation with models trained on synthetic data.

  3. 03

    Low-latency COP interface

    A common operational picture, always current, for the people deciding on the ground.

  4. 04

    Tactical resource recommendation

    Automatic suggestions for allocating resources against the identified risk.

  5. 05

    Modular dual-use architecture

    Independent components, reusable in both civil and defence contexts.
