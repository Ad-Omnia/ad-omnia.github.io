    Privacy notice

    This website presents Ad Omnia, an Informatics Engineering Project at the University of Aveiro (2026/2027). It has no accounts, no forms, no analytics and no cookies. This page explains the little personal data it does involve, as required by Art. 13 GDPR.

    Last updated: 29/10/2026 Contact: adomnia.inperpetuum@gmail.com

    Who is responsible

    The five members of the Ad Omnia team (Bernardo Reis, Diogo Silva, Fernando Santos, Guilherme Martins and Miguel Neto) are jointly responsible for this website and act as controllers of the personal data on it. Write to adomnia.inperpetuum@gmail.com for any question or request about your data.

    What data this website involves

    Data Whose Why Legal basis
    Names, project roles, university emails, GitHub and LinkedIn profiles (team page, communication plan) Team members Present the team and let people contact it Consent of each member, which can be withdrawn at any time (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR)
    Names and roles (team page) Supervisors and advisors Credit the people who guide the project Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR)
    Names in the list of participants (meeting minutes) Team members Record who took part in each decision Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR)
    IP address, browser and requested page, in server logs Visitors Deliver the pages and keep the service secure Handled by GitHub, which hosts the site (see below)

    Student numbers are not published. People outside the team who take part in a meeting are referred to by their role, unless they agree to be named.

    Hosting

    The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub receives your IP address when you open a page and may keep it in its logs for security purposes, under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. GitHub is a US company; transfers to the US rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework (Decision (EU) 2023/1795). The team does not see or keep these logs. The repository link at the top of every page also asks GitHub's API (api.github.com) for the repository's public star and fork counts, so GitHub receives your IP address from that request too.

    Fonts and the PDF viewer used for the slides and the brand book are served from this site, so opening a page does not contact any other provider.

    What is stored on your device

    The site keeps your light or dark theme choice and whether the side panels are collapsed in your browser's local storage. This stays on your device, is never sent to anyone and is strictly necessary for a feature you chose, so no consent is needed (Art. 5(3) ePrivacy Directive, Art. 5 Lei n.º 41/2004). Clearing your browser data removes it.

    Videos

    Brand videos are embedded from youtube-nocookie.com and load only when you press play. Until then nothing is sent to YouTube. Once you play a video, YouTube receives your IP address under its own privacy policy.

    How long the data is kept

    Team, supervisor and meeting data stays on the site for the duration of the project and as part of its public record afterwards, unless the person asks for it to be removed. Removed data may remain in the history of the site's Git repository.

    Your rights

    You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict its use or to object to it, and you can withdraw your consent at any time (Arts. 15–21 GDPR, Art. 7(3) GDPR). Write to adomnia.inperpetuum@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Portuguese supervisory authority, the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD).
